Spring planting on the balcony

Plant tomatoes and basil on the balcony in mid-May, once the night frosts are over. Tomatoes need a sunny spot and a support stick, and they should be watered evenly at the roots. Basil can grow in a pot on the windowsill, and pinching the tips makes the plant bushier. Add compost to the pots and check the leaves for pests once a week.
