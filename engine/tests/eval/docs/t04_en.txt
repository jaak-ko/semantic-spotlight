Nicotine replacement therapy

Nicotine replacement therapy helps reduce cravings when you quit smoking. A patch gives a steady dose across the day, while gum or lozenges help with sudden urges. Treatment usually lasts from three months to half a year. Side effects can include headaches and sleep problems. Ask a pharmacist or your health centre whether it suits you, and combine it with a plan for the triggers that make you reach for a cigarette.
