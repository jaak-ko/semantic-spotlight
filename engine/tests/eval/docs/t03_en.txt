Hiking in Lapland with a tent

A three-day hike through Pallas-Yllastunturi National Park, sleeping in a tent. Pack sturdy hiking boots, rain gear and a light tent that you pitch in the shelter of a slope against the wind. The overnight spot is by Lake Hannukuru and the route is about 25 kilometres. Bring a gas stove, a warm sleeping bag and check the weather before setting off. Reindeer are common, so keep food hung from a tree away from the tent.
