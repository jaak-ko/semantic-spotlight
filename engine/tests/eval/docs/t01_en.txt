Cardamom buns

This is my mother's old recipe for cardamom buns that work every time. You need 500 g wheat flour, 5 dl milk, 50 g fresh yeast, 1 dl sugar, 100 g butter and about two teaspoons of ground cardamom. Knead the dough and let it rise for two hours somewhere warm. Shape into knots, brush with egg, and bake at 225 C for about eight minutes. I keep this with the cinnamon roll recipes in the kitchen notebook.
